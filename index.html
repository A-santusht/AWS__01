Why Does It Matter?

Pollution, climate change and too much waste are harming our planet. Protecting the environment means keeping our air, water and land clean for future generations.

Pollution

Air, water and soil pollution affects health and wildlife.

Climate Change

Rising temperatures are causing floods, droughts and melting ice.

Deforestation

Cutting trees reduces oxygen and destroys animal homes.

Waste Management

Follow the 3 R's: Reduce, Reuse and Recycle. Segregating waste at home is the first step.

Green Wet / Biodegradable Food scraps, vegetable peels, leaves

Eco-Friendly Practices

Save Energy

  • Switch off lights and fans when not in use
  • Use LED bulbs
  • Unplug chargers

Save Water

  • Fix leaking taps
  • Turn off tap while brushing
  • Collect rainwater

Reduce Plastic

  • Carry a cloth bag
  • Use a steel water bottle
  • Say no to single-use plastic

Travel Smart

  • Walk or cycle for short distances
  • Use public transport
  • Carpool when possible
